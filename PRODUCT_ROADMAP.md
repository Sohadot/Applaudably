# Product Roadmap

## Current decision — November 2026

Applaudably.com displays **broad creative capabilities** and clearly marked **illustrative concept examples**. It does not promise to manufacture the entire possible catalog. Only finished products with verified, individual Etsy listing URLs may be presented as purchasable. There is no onsite sales or checkout system. Custom digital design inquiries go to `studio@applaudably.com`.

**First focused commercial direction:** Nail Studio Visual Business Kit, comprising practical nail business graphics (services/pricing, booking and availability, appointment policies, and customer-facing social layouts). This is an **in-development direction**, not confirmed finished stock. Scope, product-market validation, final file formats, licensing, preview imagery, quality assurance and an Etsy listing are required before sale.

Historical kit ideas below are retained as research and concept backlog, **not an active production commitment**.

## Definition of "done" per product

1. Blueprint completed in PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md
2. Kit fully built in Canva (free-Canva compatible)
3. Actual final files exported, edited/tested and QA-verified against the detailed product specification
4. Listing accurately describes only delivered features and is published on Etsy with a verified direct listing URL
5. A genuine product entry and **Buy on Etsy** link to that exact listing, with no onsite payment or cart
6. Canonical product page, internal links and sitemap entry added only for the finished, live product
7. Availability and Etsy links rechecked periodically; unlisted products lose their buy CTA
