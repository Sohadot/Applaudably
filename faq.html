FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Understanding our capabilities, custom services and Etsy-based digital products.

Are all the examples on this site available to buy?

No. Our capabilities and concept pages illustrate potential design directions. Only products with their own published Etsy listing are offered for sale. The six legacy kit concept pages on this site are not purchasable.

Where do I buy a finished product?

On our Etsy shop. When a finished product is featured on this website, its purchase button will lead to the specific Etsy listing. There is no onsite checkout or payment form.

What software or editing account will I need?

It depends on the finished product. Check the exact Etsy listing for the file format, software requirements, editability, included elements and delivery instructions before purchase.

Can I use a digital product for a client?

Consult the license applicable to the actual product before purchase. Our usage terms page gives general guidance, but specific listing terms must be checked as well.

Can I request a design made just for me?

Yes, you can send a custom design brief to studio@applaudably.com. We discuss feasibility, scope and timing directly by email.

Are sample brands and testimonials real?

Illustrative content is not proof of a client engagement, customer experience or product outcome. Any final marketing design must use accurate, authorized content.

What about refunds or order problems?

Review the refund and cancellation details on the specific Etsy listing and contact the seller through the Etsy order for assistance. Product terms and applicable buyer rights govern each purchase.

How do I access digital files after an Etsy purchase?

Follow Etsy's instructions for your order, then the file-format instructions included with that specific product. See our download help page for general guidance.

Still unsure?

Ask us directly about design projects, or contact the seller through Etsy about a specific order.
