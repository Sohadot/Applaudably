Getting your Etsy digital download.

Finished products are purchased and delivered through Etsy, not this website.

  • 1. Check the exact listing. Confirm the file formats, requirements, instructions and delivery information before purchase.
  • 2. Complete checkout on Etsy. The order and payment happen on Etsy, not applaudably.com.
  • 3. Find your download. Use Etsy's purchase and digital-file instructions; guest purchases may be handled through the confirmation email.
  • 4. Open the included instructions. Depending on the listing, your files may be PDFs, images or links to editable templates.
  • 5. Use the appropriate software. Follow the listing's stated requirements. Do not assume every future product is a Canva template.

Common hiccups

I can't find my order. Check the Etsy account used for purchase or the email associated with a guest purchase. Use Etsy's order support if needed.

A file or link isn't working. Message the seller from the relevant Etsy order with the listing name and issue.

What if the design is only a concept on this site? Concept pages are not sold and do not contain downloadable files.

Still stuck? Contact us or message us on Etsy — we'll walk you through it.
